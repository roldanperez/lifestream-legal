Privacy Policy

LifeStream · Last updated 17 November 2026

LifeStream ("we", "us") publishes short Bible verse videos to its social media pages. This policy explains what information the LifeStream app ("the App") handles when it connects to TikTok, and what we do with it.

1. Information we access from TikTok

When an authorised LifeStream account signs in with TikTok, the App receives:

  • the account's basic profile information granted during sign-in (such as display name and avatar); and
  • an access token that allows the App to upload videos to that account through the Content Posting API.

The App does not read direct messages, followers, contacts, or other private account data.

2. How we use this information

  • To upload the daily verse video and its caption to the LifeStream TikTok account.
  • To show the account name and upload status to the LifeStream operator, who runs the App.

We do not sell, rent, or share TikTok user data with advertisers, data brokers, or any third party.

3. Storage and retention

Access tokens are stored only on the computer that runs the LifeStream publisher, in a local configuration file. Videos and captions are generated locally. We keep access tokens only while they are needed and delete them when the account is disconnected.

4. Your choices

You can revoke the App's access at any time from TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions. Revoking access stops all future uploads.

5. Content

Videos are assembled on the operator's computer from generated backgrounds, typography and background music. Scripture quotations are from the New International Version (NIV), copyright © 1973, 1978, 1984, 2011 by Biblica, Inc.®, used by permission.

6. Children

The App is operated for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

8. Contact
