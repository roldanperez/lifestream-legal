Privacy Policy

LifeStream · Last updated 17 November 2026

LifeStream ("we", "us") publishes short daily videos to the LifeStream Facebook Page and Instagram account. This policy explains what the LifeStream publisher handles and what we do with it.

1. What we access

The publisher runs locally on the operator's computer. To post, it uses a Facebook Page access token with the permissions pages_manage_posts, pages_read_engagement and pages_show_list, plus the connected Instagram account ID. It does not read personal messages, friend lists, or any private data about individual users.

2. How we use it

  • To upload the daily verse reel and its caption to the LifeStream Facebook Page.
  • To publish the same reel to the connected LifeStream Instagram account.
  • To display post status to the operator, who runs the publisher.

We do not sell, rent, or share any account data with advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties.

3. Storage and retention

Access tokens are stored only in a local configuration file on the computer that runs the publisher. Videos, captions and review pages are generated locally. Tokens are kept only while needed and removed when access is revoked.

4. Your choices

Access can be revoked at any time from Facebook under Settings → Business Integrations, or from Instagram/Meta account settings under Apps and Websites. Revoking access stops all future posts.

5. Content

Videos are assembled locally from generated backgrounds, typography and background music. Bible verse reels quote the New International Version (NIV®), copyright © 1973, 1978, 1984, 2011 by Biblica, Inc.®, used by permission, and carry the required notice in each video and caption. Separate "message" reels are original LifeStream notes and do not quote or attribute Scripture.

6. Children

The publisher is operated for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

8. Contact

Questions can be sent through the repository issues page: github.com/roldanperez/lifestream-legal/issues.
